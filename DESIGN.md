# Design System: Soft Minimalist Receipt

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Thesis
The design language for Collection Field is "The Soft Receipt". It preserves the thesis of a verified record but delivers it through a minimalist, airy, and organic interface. It avoids the rigidity of traditional technical UI in favor of fluid spacing and subtle elevation.

## Colors
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (Paper White)
- **Background**: `#F9FAFB` (Soft Gray 50)
- **Body Text**: `#1F2937` (Slate 800)
- **Secondary Text**: `#6B7280` (Slate 500)
- **Accent (Action)**: `#6366F1` (Indigo 500 - "The Soft Stamp")
- **Status Green**: `#10B981` (Emerald 500)
- **Status Red**: `#EF4444` (Rose 500)

## Typography
- **Primary**: Inter / Roboto (Sans-serif)
- **Data/Meta**: JetBrains Mono (Monospace) - Used only for IDs, timestamps, and coordinates.
- **Roles**:
  - `DisplayLarge`: Sans, Medium, Tracking -0.02em
  - `TitleLarge`: Sans, SemiBold
  - `BodyLarge`: Sans, Normal, Line Height 1.5
  - `LabelSmall`: Mono, SemiBold, Tracking 0.05em (All-caps for meta)

## Spacing & Rhythm
- Base Unit: `8dp` (Softer than 4dp)
- Radius: `12dp` to `16dp` for containers.
- Padding: Generous whitespace around content.

## Components
- **Dashboard Cards**: No borders, very subtle organic shadows or light tonal backgrounds.
- **Primary Buttons**: Rounded (full radius or 12dp), solid color with soft elevation.
- **Dividers**: Very light horizontal lines or just whitespace.

## Motion
- **Fluid**: Smooth alpha fades and gentle scale-up transitions.
- **Subtle**: Feedback is felt through color shifts and micro-translations.
